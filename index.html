Sebastian Reinartz

Hello! My name is Sebastian Reinartz and I am a PhD student at the Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH).

My research interests are education and labor economics, with a focus on the skills of teachers and students and how they are rewarded in the labor market. In my current projects I study the cognitive skills of teachers and their relationship to student achievement, the wage returns to adaptability, and the formation of higher-order skills over the life course.
